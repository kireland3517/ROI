    Pre-List Plan uses your address only to build your Seller Action Plan. We do not require an account to generate your first plan.

    Correct what is wrong

    Local market context
    Recorded nearby sales appear only when data is available for your market. They provide context — not a value estimate.
    Source-labeled output
